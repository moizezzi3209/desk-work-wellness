Desk Work Wellness

Understand your working week.

A simple personal reflection on desk-work sitting and the movement that interrupts it. Record what happens, notice your patterns and choose one achievable change.

This is an awareness tool, not a fitness tracker or health-risk assessment. It focuses on sitting during desk work. It does not measure all the time you spend sitting during the day.

My desk-work sitting tracker

Record the approximate hours you spent sitting during desk work each day. Include computer work, reading, writing, studying, paperwork and seated meetings. Do not include commuting, television, meals or other non-desk sitting in this activity.

Your best estimate in hours. Enter 0 only if you had no uninterrupted sitting period.

My daily movement log

For each day, record movement that actually interrupted or replaced desk-work sitting. Each occasion should be counted once. Leave fields blank if you have not recorded them; enter 0 when there were none.

Which box should I use? If getting up is part of an activity you were already doing, record it as everyday/task-related movement. If you deliberately pause desk work to move, record it as a desk-work movement break. Choose the closest description; do not overthink the reason. The same type of activity, such as walking or stretching, may fit either box depending on the occasion.

4. Explore a change

Small opportunities to replace some sitting with movement can be part of a working day.

Ideas to consider
Between tasksWalk for a few minutes after completing a task, where practical.
During suitable callsStand or walk during a phone call when it is safe and appropriate.
Between meetingsUse the transition to move rather than remain seated, when possible.
At a natural pauseStretch, change position or take a short movement break.

There is no rigid hourly break prescription here. Choose opportunities that fit your work, environment and individual circumstances.
