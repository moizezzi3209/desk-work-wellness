♧  Guide: how to record movement

Choose the type of movement based on why you got up.

🚶 Everyday / task-related movement

Movement while carrying out usual activities — getting water, washroom trips, stairs or walking between work areas.

🙆 Deliberate movement breaks

Movement deliberately introduced to interrupt sitting — a short walk, stretching, mobility or a planned movement pause.

Look for opportunities to move

  • Between tasks: Walk for a few minutes.
  • During suitable calls: Stand or walk, where practical.
  • Between meetings: Use the transition to move.
  • At a natural pause: Stretch or change position.
ⓘ There is no rigid hourly break prescription. Choose opportunities that fit your work and circumstances.
