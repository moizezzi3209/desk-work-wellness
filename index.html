A useful distinction

Being physically active does not necessarily mean you sit less. Your sitting pattern and exercise habits tell us different things.

Other sedentary time, such as commuting or recreational screen time, is not included in your desk-work sitting total. You can observe it separately if you wish.

Your seven-day desk-work sitting record

Record sitting during computer work, reading, writing, studying, paperwork and seated meetings. Exclude commuting, television, meals and other non-desk sitting in this first activity.

Log movement breaks during desk work

Count occasions when you deliberately interrupted sitting with movement. Record the number for each day. This is separate from general physical-activity minutes.

Privacy and data controls

Your records are stored locally in this browser. They are not sent to a server. Clearing browser data, using private browsing or changing devices may make them unavailable. Keep a JSON backup.
